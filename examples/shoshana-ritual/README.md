# Shoshana Ritual Homepage

This is the "ritual homepage" concept built for the Shoshana mysticism project.

## The Concept

Instead of an over-comprehensive homepage (35 chapters at once), the homepage
becomes a LIVING RITUAL: one thing at a time, changing with the time of day,
lunar phase, and season.

## The Problem It Solves

AI-generated websites tend to be over-comprehensive — they try to show
everything at once. This makes them feel like encyclopedias, not experiences.

The ritual approach transforms this: focus on ONE thing, give it space,
let it breathe, and make it change with time.

## Files

- `NewHomePage.tsx` — the ritual homepage (6 moments)
- `NewHomePage.css` — intimate, breathing design
- `cosmic.ts` — lunar and seasonal awareness
- `RitualTarotCardPage.tsx` — ONE tarot card, deeply
- `RitualZodiacPage.tsx` — ONE zodiac sign, deeply

## The 6 Ritual Moments

1. Opening line (changes with time of day)
2. The daily card (one card, deeply)
3. The whisper (one proverb)
4. The cosmic moment (lunar phase + season)
5. The story (one soul story)
6. Closing line (changes with time of day)

## Relevance to Forge

This demonstrates a design principle that could inform Forge UI:
**focus over comprehensiveness**. A CI/CD dashboard that shows the ONE
thing that matters right now, rather than everything at once.
